# 🏃‍♂️ The Maze Runner Trilogy

Welcome to my first repository! This `README.md` highlights one of my favorite dystopian sci-fi film trilogies: **The Maze Runner**, adapted from the novels by *James Dashner* and directed by Wes Ball.

> *"If you ain't scared, you ain't human."* — **Alby**

---

## 🎬 Film Overview

Set in a post-apocalyptic world ravaged by the **Flare virus**, a group of teenagers with erased memories must survive deadly trials orchestrated by the mysterious organization `WCKD` *(World In Catastrophe: Killzone Experiment Department)*.

## 🧩 Core Characters

- **Thomas** – The curious newcomer who becomes a Runner and leads the escape from the Glade.
- **Newt** – The loyal second-in-command and emotional anchor of the Gladers (*"Great, we're all bloody inspired"*).
- **Minho** – The fearless Keeper of the Runners with unmatched stamina and sharp humor.
- **Teresa** – The final arrival in the Glade who holds a complicated past with ~~WICKED~~ `WCKD`.
